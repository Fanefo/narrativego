Privacy Policy & Data Protection (GDPR)

Last updated: October 12, 2026 • EU Regulation 2016/679 Compliance

We fully respect each visitor's fundamental right to privacy. This policy transparently explains how narrativeGO (operated by Krisztina Hegedűs, acting as Data Controller) processes and protects personal data.

1. Data Controller & Contact Information

The controller responsible for processing data collected via this website is Krisztina Hegedűs (brand narrativeGO). For any privacy inquiries, reach out directly at: krisztina.seoux@gmail.com.

2. Data Collected & Purpose

We strictly adhere to the GDPR Data Minimization Principle:

  • Contact & Audit Forms: Name, business email, website URL, and project message.
  • Purpose: Responding to consultation requests, evaluating technical prerequisites, and providing tailored proposals.
  • Legal Basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (steps taken prior to entering into a contract) and Art. 6(1)(a) (explicit consent given when submitting the form).

3. Data Retention Period

Data submitted via contact forms is retained only as long as necessary for active commercial communication (maximum 12 months after the last interaction, unless an ongoing partnership contract is executed).

4. Infrastructure Security & Hosting

This website is hosted on high-performance, secure infrastructure located within the European Union (256-bit SSL/TLS encryption, server-level WAF firewall, and proactive DDoS shielding).

5. Your Rights Under the GDPR

You are entitled to all rights provided by EU Regulation 2016/679:

  1. Right of Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  2. Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate information.
  3. Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request complete deletion of your records.
  4. Right to Restrict or Object to Processing.
  5. Right to lodge a complaint with the relevant supervisory data protection authority.
