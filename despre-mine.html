Personal Background

I'm Krisztina Hegedűs. Obsessed with data, passionate about stories that convert.

I believe that the best search optimization strategies do not try to trick algorithms; they help real people discover the exact answers they were searching for.

My Journey

Why I Created narrativeGO

I began working in organic search out of a desire to bring clarity, honesty, and analytical discipline to an industry overflowing with vague promises. Over the years, I watched too many brands spend massive budgets on hundreds of thin articles or questionable backlink packages that failed to generate a single paying customer.

I launched narrativeGO with a singular mission: to unite the analytical rigor of technical SEO with the craft of commercial narrative storytelling. When your platform has an immaculate technical foundation and content that people genuinely read from top to bottom, high rankings become a natural byproduct, not an accidental spike.

Operating independently gives me the freedom to partner with a carefully selected number of clients each quarter. That ensures every engagement benefits from my undivided attention, bespoke strategy, and authentic senior commitment.
